## Parking Notes

- **No building confirmed motorcycle parking.** Must ask every agent.
- Car spaces are typically "by separate negotiation" = extra cost, limited supply.
- Planning precedent: Wandsworth application 2023/1142 (Nine Elms area) includes basement motorcycle bays alongside car/cycle — some Nine Elms schemes do have them. https://planning-records.uk/wandsworth/2023/20231142
- Central London on-street parking is residents'-permit controlled (Westminster, Camden, RBKC, Wandsworth/Lambeth); external garages in Fitzrovia/Covent Garden are expensive → favours buildings with their own basement parking (Nine Elms, Chelsea Creek).

## Key Questions to Ask Every Building

1. Is there a **motorcycle bay**, or can a motorcycle use a car bay? Cost?
2. Car space: included, or separately negotiated? Monthly cost? Waitlist?
3. Floor-to-ceiling windows in this specific unit?
4. Washer-dryer combo or separate washer + dryer?
5. Any 1 bed + study units coming up?
6. Rent quoted pcm or pw? Deposit weeks? Furnished vs unfurnished price?
