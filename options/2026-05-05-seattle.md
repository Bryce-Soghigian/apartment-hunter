### Key Takeaways

1. **AMLI Arc is the clear frontrunner** — only building with a verified 2BR under $4,700 that confirms all hard requirements (F2C, W/D, gym, rooftop). The 2BR C3 at $4,036/mo with 6 weeks free is a strong deal. Also has a music studio and co-working.

2. **McKenzie has the best rooftop in Seattle** — 360° wraparound deck, spa, Wild Ginger restaurant. But in-unit W/D is unconfirmed. Call to verify.

3. **The Ayer is the tallest** at 45 stories with likely F2C throughout, but needs unit-level verification.

4. **Seattle is dramatically cheaper than NYC** — 1BRs start at $1,900–$3,100 vs. $4,300–$5,000+ in Manhattan. 2BRs available under $4,100.

5. **Den/office configs are rare** — unlike NYC, Seattle buildings don't commonly market 1BR+den. The larger 1BR floor plans (900+ sq ft at AMLI Arc) may serve as a de facto office setup, or go 2BR.
